A. Popover Card

Small card drops down from the ? button. Stays open until clicked away. Lightweight, doesn't block the calendar.

Pros

  • Non-intrusive
  • Quick to scan
  • Stays near context

Cons

  • Limited space for bilingual content
  • May overlap calendar cells

B. Modal Dialog

Centered overlay (like filter modal). Plenty of room for bilingual content, sections, keyboard shortcuts. Follows existing .cal-filter-modal pattern.

Pros

  • Room for thorough bilingual content
  • Matches existing pattern
  • Accessible (focus trap, ESC)

Cons

  • Interrupts workflow
  • Heavier feel for a help reference

C. Modal with Language Tabs

Compact modal with English/עברית tab switcher. Auto-selects tab based on current locale. Full content in both languages without overwhelming the user. Recommended.

Pros

  • Both languages fully covered
  • Clean — one language at a time
  • Auto-selects based on locale
  • Room for all content

Cons

  • Slightly heavier than popover
