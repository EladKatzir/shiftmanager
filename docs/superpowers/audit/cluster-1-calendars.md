# Cluster 1 — Calendars — Audit Report

**Date:** 2026-08-03  |  **Branch:** `dev` (working tree had uncommitted Tabs-admin changes)  
**Method:** read-only multi-agent audit (recon -> 10 dimension auditors -> adversarial refuters), 
plus main-loop runtime verification on an isolated Release instance (:5080, copy of `app.db`).

## Executive summary — READ THE SCOPE BEFORE THE NUMBERS

This audit surfaced **132 raw findings (10 critical, 34 high, 68 medium, 20 low)** across the
Calendars cluster. The dominant theme is a single recurring defect shape: **authorization is
checked against the CALLER's own company/scope rather than the TARGET's**, which yields
cross-molecule and cross-tenant write access from ordinary seats.

**Confidence is bounded and this bound is load-bearing:**

- The run **hit the session token limit partway through**. 22 of 52 agents errored.
- **2 of 10 audit dimensions never ran at all**: `chores` (the whole `/Calendar/Chores` feature)
  and `ux-rtl-l10n` (the entire localization/RTL/contrast sweep). There are ZERO findings for
  those surfaces — that is absence of evidence, NOT evidence of absence.
- Only **40 of 132** findings were sent to adversarial refuters (top-5 per dimension by severity);
  **92 findings were never independently checked** and may contain false positives.
- The synthesis agent also failed, so this report was assembled mechanically from the raw journal.
- Everything here is **code-reading** except where explicitly marked RUNTIME-VERIFIED.

Of the 40 findings that reached refuters, **15 were CONFIRMED** and **6 were REFUTED**
(the remainder's verdict agents died on the session limit).

### C1-F01 (CRITICAL) — `ViewAllShifts` is a fully provisioned grant that NOTHING enforces

- **Kind:** design-flaw + implementation-bug (the grant exists; no code reads it)
- **Evidence:** seeded at `Data/SeedData/GrantTypeSeed.cs:21` (DefaultScope=Molecule); granted
  ExpandToMolecule to Lead/BRDirector/Director/MoleculeAdmin at `Data/SeedData/RoleTemplateSeed.cs:310`
  with the comment *"Lead/מפ"צ sees shifts across all companies in their molecule"*; both seeded
  Alhut leads hold it (`ViewAllShifts, MoleculeId=1`). **Zero** live non-seed, non-test references
  in `.cs`/`.cshtml`; not in the dynamic key list at `Services/GrantService.cs:103-106`.
- **Consumer that should read it:** `Pages/Calendar/Team.cshtml.cs:46` hardcodes
  `ViewShiftsGrantKey = "ViewShifts"` (company-scoped for Lead), so the desk picker collapses.
- **Runtime proof (7 personas on :5080):** Owner 36 desks, Director 10 desks, Alhut lead (Hir) **1**,
  Alhut lead (City) **1**, Manager 1, Member 1, NoGrants 1. The Director and the leads are in the
  SAME molecule — the Director sees all 10 desks, the leads see 1.
- **Why it matters:** leads hold `AssignShifts` at molecule scope, so they can ASSIGN people across
  10 desks they cannot VIEW. Fixing only the 'add table' dialog would have produced a chooser with
  one option.
- **Fix (user-approved, option A):** give `ViewAllShifts` a single enforcement point —
  `IGrantService.GetShiftVisibleCompanyIdsAsync` = union(ViewShifts, ViewAllShifts) — and have Team
  use it in BOTH `OnGetAsync` and `OnPostAddViewAsync` (or the POST will 403 what the GET offered).
